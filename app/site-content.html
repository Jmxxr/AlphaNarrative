11 / FREQUENTLY ASKED

Before we build.

What types of businesses do you work with?+

We work with ambitious SMEs, growing companies and institutions that need stronger digital products, operations or automation.

How much does a project cost?+

Investment depends on scope, integrations, security and complexity. After discovery, you receive a transparent proposal with deliverables and milestone payments.

How long does development take?+

A focused website may take weeks; a connected business system takes longer. We provide a realistic roadmap after understanding the work.

Will you maintain the system after launch?+

Yes. Monthly support can include monitoring, backups, security updates, small improvements and intelligence reviews.

Who owns the finished product?+

Ownership and licensing are defined clearly in the service agreement. Client accounts, access and handover responsibilities are documented.
